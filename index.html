Daniel Frey


PhD candidate at Technical University of Munich (TUM), affiliated with the Chair of Biomedical Physics and the Munich Institute of Biomedical Engineering, in collaboration with the Chair of AI in Healthcare and Medicine.

My research focuses on the clinical translation of human-scale dark-field computed tomography (DFCT), an X-ray modality based on small-angle scattering with strong potential for improved lung diagnostics. I am developing algorithms for image quality enhancement, integrating physical domain knowledge into modern machine learning, with a particular interest in continuous image representations.

I hold Bachelor's degrees in Biochemistry and Physics from TUM, where I also went on to complete the Biomedical Engineering and Medical Physics (BEMP) Master's program. Alongside my studies, I worked at the TÜV SÜD electrical safety department and Morphometry Group at TUM University Hospital, collecting both practical skills and experience in deep learning workflows.
